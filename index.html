Our Story

从重庆到悉尼

From the backstreets of Chongqing to Level 4 of Macquarie Centre — one bowl at a time.

Born in the backstreets of Chongqing, 趙崽兒 was never meant to be fancy. It started as a simple noodle stall — one man, one pot of chili oil, and a belief that a great bowl of noodles doesn't need a speech. It just needs to be right.

Now at Macquarie Centre, we bring that same street-side energy to Sydney. Our noodles are still made fresh. Our chili oil is still house-blended. The space is sleeker — warm terracotta walls, sage green counters, soft spherical lights — but the soul? Unchanged. Walk in, grab a bowl, and taste Chongqing the way it's meant to be.

We keep it simple. No fusion, no fuss — just seriously good noodles. Vegans welcome. Students welcome. Everyone welcome. That's the 崽儿 way.
